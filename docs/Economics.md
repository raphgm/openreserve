# Economics & Tokenomics

The economic model of OpenReserve is designed to secure the network, reward honest behavior, and align incentives across validators, delegators, and the core protocol treasury.

## The Reserve Asset (ORP)

- **Utility**: ORP is the native asset used to pay for transaction fees, deploy smart contracts, and stake for consensus security.
- **Deflationary Mechanisms**: A portion of every transaction fee is permanently burned, applying deflationary pressure against the inflationary issuance.

## Inflation and Rewards
- **Base Inflation**: Set at 2% annualized.
- **Dynamic Yield**: The staking yield is a function of the total network percentage currently staked. If a lower percentage of the total supply is staked, the yield per staker increases, incentivizing more actors to secure the network.

## Slashing
Validators that double-sign blocks or suffer extended downtime are penalized (slashed). A portion of their staked ORP (and their delegators' ORP) is burned, protecting the network from malicious actors and ensuring high uptime.
